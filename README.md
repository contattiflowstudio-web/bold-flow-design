# FLOW STUDIO HOMEPAGE

Design and build a complete, modern, high-converting marketing website for Flow Studio, a web design and digital marketing agency. The site must feel premium, bold, and cutting-edge — like the work of an agency that truly masters the digital world.

🎨 Visual Identity & Design System

Color Palette: Deep navy blue (#0A0F2C), rich purple (#5B21B6 / #7C3AED), electric violet (#A855F7), clean white (#FFFFFF), and soft light-grey (#F3F4F6) for backgrounds

Typography: Use a bold, geometric sans-serif for headings (like Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) and a clean readable font for body text

Style: Dark-themed hero and sections, with glassmorphism cards, subtle gradient glows, animated grain texture or mesh gradient backgrounds, and smooth micro-animations on scroll

Mood: Sophisticated, creative, trustworthy, and forward-thinking

📄 Page Structure & Sections

1. Navigation Bar

Logo "Flow Studio" on the left (text-based with a subtle flow/wave icon)

Links: Home, Services, Why Digital, Portfolio, Contact

A glowing CTA button: "Start Your Project"

Sticky, with blur/glass effect on scroll

2. Hero Section

Full-screen dark hero with an animated mesh gradient background (deep navy + purple tones)

Bold headline: "We Build Digital Experiences That Convert"

Subheadline: "Web design, social media management and digital marketing — all in one studio."

Two CTA buttons: "See Our Work" (primary, glowing purple) and "Talk to Us" (ghost/outline)

A subtle animated element (floating abstract shapes, particles, or a looping code/design mockup visual)

3. Services Section

Title: "What We Do"

Three main service cards with icons, title and short description:

🌐 Website Creation — Custom, fast, and responsive websites built to impress and convert

📱 Social Media Management — Strategy, content creation, and growth across all platforms

📈 Digital Marketing — SEO, paid ads, and campaigns that bring real business results

Cards with glassmorphism style and a purple glow on hover

4. "Why Your Business Needs a Digital Identity" Section

Educative, persuasive section explaining to potential clients why having a professional website and digital presence is essential in today's market

Use a split layout: left side with strong headline + paragraph text, right side with 3–4 stat/fact cards (e.g., "75% of users judge a company's credibility based on its website", "Businesses with a website grow 2x faster", etc.)

Warm but confident tone — guide the reader toward understanding the value

End with a soft CTA: "Don't be invisible online. Let's change that."

5. Portfolio Section

Title: "Our Work Speaks for Itself"

A filterable grid (filter by: All, Websites, Social Media, Branding)

6 placeholder project cards, each with:

A project thumbnail (use stylized placeholder images)

Project name and category tag

A hover overlay with a "View Project" button

Clean masonry or uniform card grid layout

6. Social Proof / Trust Section

A short strip with: client logos (placeholder), a headline like "Trusted by brands that want to grow"

Below: 2–3 testimonial cards with avatar, name, company, and quote

7. Call-to-Action Section

Full-width dark section with a bold gradient background

Headline: "Ready to elevate your brand?"

Subtext: "Let's build something great together."

Large CTA button: "Get a Free Consultation"

8. Footer

Logo + short tagline

Navigation links

Social media icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance)

"© 2025 Flow Studio. All rights reserved."

⚙️ Technical Requirements

Fully responsive (mobile-first)

Smooth scroll behavior and scroll-triggered fade-in animations

All sections cleanly separated with consistent spacing

Use Tailwind CSS utility classes throughout

Component-based structure (each section as its own component)

No placeholder lorem ipsum — write real, compelling copy for all sections
